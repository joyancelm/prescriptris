Fill a horizontal row with any pill types to clear it. Clear 10 rows to level up.

Space / Drop: place the piece and clear rows.
B / Bottle + drop: place it, collect matching squares first, then settle pills and clear rows.

Squares remain available unless their rows clear. Bottling a 4×4 pays more than four 2×2 batches.
