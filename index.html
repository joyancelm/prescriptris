A free pharmacy puzzle game

Prescriptris is a free, single-player falling-block puzzle game set in a retro pharmacy. Stack pill pieces, clear horizontal rows, and collect matching squares with the bottling mechanic.

Play online on desktop, tablet, or phone. Use a keyboard or the on-screen touch controls. There is no download or account required.

How to play Prescriptris

  1. Stack and clear. Move and rotate each falling piece. Fill a complete horizontal row with any pill types to clear it. Keep the stack below the top of the tray.
  2. Build matching batches. Make a 2×2, 3×3, or 4×4 square of the same pill type: red/cream capsules, white round tablets, or blue oblong caplets.
  3. Choose when to bottle. Space places a piece and clears rows normally. B drops the piece and collects matching squares first, largest batches first. A successful bottle collection settles loose pills before clearing full rows. Row clears can remove pills from an uncollected square.

A 2×2 batch earns 80 points, a 3×3 earns 240, and a 4×4 earns 600, multiplied by your level. Clear 10 rows to level up. Complete the current prescription for an extra bonus.

Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D to move; ↑/X to rotate clockwise; Z to rotate back; ↓/S to soft drop; Space to hard drop; B to bottle and drop; C/Shift to hold; P/Escape to pause.

Phone or tablet: use the arrow, rotate, and Drop buttons beneath the tray. Hold/swap and Bottle + drop have their own buttons. Choose Continue after an automatic pause.

Prescriptris questions

Is Prescriptris free to play?
Yes. Play the full browser game for free, without signing up or installing an app.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes. Prescriptris has a responsive layout and on-screen touch controls. Desktop players can use the keyboard.
Do I need pharmacy knowledge?
No. You match three pill shapes and colors. The game does not test medication names or dosages.
How is bottling different from clearing rows?
Rows clear when every cell is filled, whatever the pill types. Bottling collects matching square batches when you choose Bottle + drop or press B.
Is my high score saved?
Your best score is saved in this browser when local storage is available. It is not a shared online leaderboard. Clearing browser data can remove it.
Can I turn off sound and spinning effects?
Yes. Use Sound and CRT in the toolbar. The game also respects your system’s reduced-motion preference.
