# Prescriptris v2 artwork

The PNG assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The SVG wordmark is original vector lettering built locally with `scripts/build-wordmark.js`; regenerate it with `npm run art:logo`. The active game does not use the legacy sprite directory. Generated PNG originals remain in the Codex generated-images folder; the game references only the project-local copies here.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `prescriptris-wordmark.svg` | Original 5×7 module block lettering with a warm face, light outline, and blue extrusion; crisp at desktop and mobile sizes |
| `pharmacy.png` | Walnut/teal apothecary background, darkened by CSS outside the tray |
| `pharmacist.png` | Two equal horizontal character frames: idle and celebration; genuine alpha transparency |
| `medication-pills.png` | Active pill sprites: red/cream capsule, white scored round tablet, blue scored oblong caplet; three equal frames with alpha transparency |
| `pill-bottle-atlas.png` | Bottle sprite retained for collection effects; the three earlier colored capsule tiles are no longer used |

The active medication atlas has three square frames. `game.js` caches 12 crisp rotations per pill and crops each rotation to its visible alpha bounds. The longest visible dimension fills 98% of the available cell interior while keeping the source aspect ratio. This makes the round tablet larger without stretching it, and keeps spinning capsules and caplets inside their cells. Shape and score lines distinguish the three pill types in addition to color. A subtle neutral tray-cell backing keeps the tetromino footprint readable without adding a colored square to the artwork. Preview shapes are centered from their occupied cells; hold, next, locked, active, and ghost pieces share the same spin frames. Spin freezes on pause/game over and respects motion settings. Image-failure fallbacks also use the three different medication shapes and cached rotations. The source PNG is unchanged. Generated character frames remain at a fixed size outside gameplay panels.

The earlier four-object atlas has unequal object spacing. Only its bottle is still sampled, using a normalized rectangle fitted to its actual bounds. Bottle effects and pharmacist frames are contained inside their destination rectangles without stretching their source proportions.

No external images or fonts are fetched during play. `game.js` contains simple code-drawn fallbacks for image failure, not replacements for the generated artwork.

The interface uses a locally bundled Press Start 2P font from Google's official fonts repository. Its source, license, and font log are in `../fonts/`. The custom PRESCRIPTRIS wordmark uses its own vector glyphs; it is not a font image or a traced logo.

## Exact generation prompts

### medication-pills.png — active replacement

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original transparent medication-pill sprite atlas for Prescriptris, a 16-bit retro pharmacy falling-block game. Create a wide 3:1 canvas with EXACTLY THREE equally sized square frames in one horizontal row, each pill strictly centered within its own third. The subjects must be three clearly DIFFERENT recognizable real-world medication dosage forms, NOT three variations of one capsule: LEFT: glossy red-and-ivory two-piece hard gelatin capsule, horizontal with a seam at the center, rounded ends, 2.3:1 width-to-height; CENTER: a round white/off-white pressed tablet, circular coin-like silhouette, subtle beveled edge, a single straight recessed score line crossing its face diagonally, slight visible thickness along lower edge; RIGHT: a blue oblong compressed caplet tablet, NOT a capsule, solid blue coating with one recessed score line in its middle, elongated rounded-rectangle silhouette oriented diagonally upward at 35 degrees. All three isolated on a genuinely transparent background with transparent space between frames. Use the SAME clean hand-drawn 16-bit pixel-art style for all: crisp square pixels, dark teal-charcoal outlines, restrained ivory highlights, clear small-scale silhouettes, coherent top-left light source, no anti-aliased fringes. Each subject occupies about 80 percent of its frame width or diagonal span with consistent visual weight when shown at 28 pixels. Red capsule, white round tablet, blue caplet. No colored square tile backings, no boxes, no frames, no floor, no bottle, no text, no imprints, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no stock markings, no extra pills. Original game artwork, not medical photography.

### pharmacy.png

Warm dark walnut shelves lined with small amber bottles with cream labels, deep forest teal woodwork, muted mint accents, a simple wooden counter across bottom, a small hanging cream pharmacy cross sign. Consistent square pixels, hand-placed pixel-art appearance, limited palette, warm restrained lighting, cozy late-night pharmacy. Background scenery only: no people, no pills in foreground, no text, no logo, no watermark, no CRT overlay. Keep the middle area quiet and dark because a separate game board UI will be layered over it. Crisp pixel art, not realistic, not blurry.

### pharmacist.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original transparent pixel-art character sprite sheet for Prescriptris, a cozy 16-bit pharmacy puzzle game. Exactly two equal rectangular frames side by side on a transparent background, separated at the exact center. In EACH frame the SAME friendly adult pharmacist with short dark hair, round glasses, cream lab coat over deep forest-teal shirt, shown from waist up behind a small warm walnut counter with a single amber medicine bottle with cream label. Frame 1 relaxed smiling, hands by bottle. Frame 2 happy celebration, one hand raised with a small mint check-mark sparkle overhead. Character same size and position in each frame, generous transparent margins on each side of each frame, entire subject fully contained. Consistent hand-placed 16-bit pixel art, strong dark outlines, square pixels, limited warm amber, cream, forest-teal and muted mint palette. Flat front view, clear silhouette suitable for shrinking to 120 pixels tall. No full scene or shelves, no text, no watermark, no lettering. Real transparent background.
